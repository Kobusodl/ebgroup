About Us

Built on experience. Focused on quality.

EB Construction is a Johannesburg-based construction company specialising in residential, commercial, and civil construction projects across Gauteng.

Our Story

Founded in 2009, EB Construction started with a vision to provide reliable and professional construction services with a personal touch.

Over the years, we have grown from small residential projects to a trusted partner for medium to large-scale developments in the residential, commercial and civil sectors.

Our growth is built on hard work, integrity, and a focus on building long-term client relationships.
